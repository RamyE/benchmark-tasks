# Pressurize a 1-gallon pump sprayer and spot-spray a weed hidden under an overhanging hosta leaf

Instruction: `Pump the 1-gallon garden sprayer into its green 4 to 8 PSI low-drift range, hold the overhanging hosta leaf back off the dandelion weed in the planter box, spot-spray the dandelion crown for at least 2 seconds without wetting the hosta leaf, and return the shut-off wand to its holster.`

## Occupational context and two-arm skill
Landscaping and groundskeeping workers (`O*NET 37-3011.00`, `1,188,600` US workers, 2025 — core task: *"Mix and spray or spread fertilizers, herbicides, or insecticides onto grass, shrubs, or trees, using hand or automatic sprayers or spreaders"*) and crop, nursery, and greenhouse farmworkers (`O*NET 45-2092.00`, `546,800` US workers, 2025 — *"Plant, spray, weed, fertilize, water, and prune plants, shrubs, and trees, using gardening tools"*) spot-treat broadleaf weeds in ornamental beds every day. Treating a weed tucked beneath an ornamental plant's foliage with a `Chapin 20000` 1-gallon compression sprayer requires two distinct bimanual phases:
1. **Tank hold-down and Boyle's-law pneumatic pumping**: Filled with a `500 mL` spot-weeding batch (`3,285 cm³` air headspace, `1.16 kg` = `11.4 N` total tank weight), the unclamped HDPE tank weighs less than the `13.8 N` Viton piston-cup seal drag — pulling the top T-handle up one-handed lifts the whole tank off its containment tray. **Left hand** braces the `+y` D-handle lug firmly down (`at (21.5, 20.1, 22.5)`) while **Right hand** (`>= 19 cm` away on the `-y` end of the T-bar) pumps `>= 7` full strokes (`>= 9.0 cm` each; `11.5 cm` x `3.8 cm` bore = `130.4 cm³` swept air, adding `+0.584 PSI/stroke` by isothermal Boyle's law) to bring the `Winters PFQ800` `0–15 PSI` outlet gauge into the green `4.0–8.0 PSI` low-drift spot-spray range, finishing with the plunger seated down (`<= 2.0 cm`).
2. **Canopy retraction by petiole stem, physical trigger-lever spot-spraying, and holstering**: In the front planter trough (`(44.5, -4.0)`), a commercial `Nearly Natural` molded PE artificial hosta frond is anchored at its crown (`(43.5, +12.5, 14.8)` inside the planter — zero rear lever) so its `14 mm` petiole stem arches along `-y` (`(43.5, +4.5, 17.8)`) and its `7.6 x 10.4 cm` leaf blade drapes directly over a `9.0 cm` dandelion crown at `(43.5, -5.5, 14.2)`. **Left hand** reaches into the planter, grasps the hosta's own `14 mm` petiole stem between the crown and the leaf blade, and holds the frond swung back (`>= 38 deg`, the exact geometric angle at which the `7.6 x 10.4 cm` blade clears `(43.5, -5.5)`), while **Right hand** (`~39 cm` away) lifts the `30.5 cm` angled brass wand out of its open-top twin U-saddle holster with a light carry grip (`<= 2.0 mm` lever compression, valve shut), aims `2.5-12.0 cm` over `(43.5, -5.5, 14.2)`, physically squeezes the colliding spring-loaded shut-off trigger lever (`>= 10.0 mm`) for `>= 2.0 s` without wetting any part of the hosta leaf, relaxes the lever shut before moving away, and seats the wand back onto its twin U-saddle holster.

## Repeatable real-trial preparation and reset
1. Trials use plain clean tap water (`500 mL`) — zero chemicals — scored with agricultural `TeeJet 20301-2N` (`7.6 x 5.2 cm`) yellow water-sensitive cards, which turn bright blue instantaneously on any droplet contact. Two side-by-side `7.6 x 5.2 cm` cards (`7.6 cm` by `2 x 5.2 = 10.4 cm`) cover `100%` of both faces of the `7.6 x 10.4 cm` hosta blade so overspray anywhere on the leaf is detected.
2. Between trials (`~40 s`): vent the `Chapin 20000` relief valve to `0 PSI`, verify `500 mL` water (`1.16 kg` total tank mass, `3,285 cm³` headspace), seat the plunger down (`z = 30.2 cm`), centre the tank unclamped in the spill tray at `(21.5, 8.5)`, wipe the nozzle dry, and seat the wand onto the twin U-saddle holster at `(20.5, -31.0, 17.5)`. Fix fresh dry yellow `TeeJet 20301-2N` cards across the entire `7.6 x 10.4 cm` hosta blade (top and bottom) and flat onto the dandelion crown at `(43.5, -5.5, 14.2)`, each with full-face `3M 9474LE` double-sided tape on its back plus two small stainless binder clips at the blade's edge, so a card cannot be peeled or slid by contact; a card that is nevertheless torn, peeled or moved from its taped position scores 0 (in simulation the cards are rigid parts of the blade and rosette and cannot move). Wipe and re-tape both arm drape sleeves if any droplets landed on them.
3. Recording for scoring: besides the robot cameras, a fixed phone camera on a table tripod frames the gauge dial and the wand's shut-off handle for the whole trial. A printed index sticker on the handle body beside the trigger marks `0`, `2`, `8` and `10 mm` lever travel, so valve opening (`>= 8.0 mm`), full squeeze (`>= 10.0 mm`) and the closed valve (`<= 2.0 mm`) are read frame by frame, and the visible spray plume times the valve-open interval; the gauge needle gives the pressure at every stroke and during the spray.

## Scoring (real trial and simulation)
Judged from the trial video (robot cameras plus the fixed gauge-and-handle camera of reset step 3) and an end photo of the `TeeJet 20301-2N` cards:
- **Score 0** if at any time: any part of the `7.6 x 10.4 cm` hosta leaf is wetted (any blue spot on its full-surface `TeeJet 20301-2N` cards); the shut-off valve opens (`lever >= 8.0 mm`) while aimed `> 5.5 cm` horizontally from `(43.5, -5.5)`, outside `2.5-12.0 cm` height above the dandelion crown, or outside `3.5–8.0 PSI`; tank pressure exceeds `10.0 PSI`; the unclamped tank lifts `> 2.0 cm` off the spill tray, shifts `> 3.0 cm` from `(21.5, 8.5)`, or tilts `> 15 deg`; the hosta hinge bends `> 75 deg`; the hose is pulled taut (straight-line span from the tank tee to the handle barb `> 86.0 cm`, just under the `86.4 cm` (`34 in`) hose length); or the tank or wand leaves the table (`z < -2 cm`).
- **0.25**: `>= 7` plunger strokes (`>= 9.0 cm` travel each) raise tank gauge pressure into the `4.0–8.0 PSI` green low-drift band, and the plunger is seated down (`<= 2.0 cm` above bottom stop).
- **0.55**: also the overhanging hosta leaf is swung clear (`>= 38 deg`) while the wand is unclipped and aimed `2.5-12.0 cm` above and `<= 5.5 cm` horizontally from `(43.5, -5.5)`, with its `12 deg` spray cone clear of the leaf blade.
- **0.85**: also while the hosta leaf remains swung clear (`>= 38 deg`), the colliding wand shut-off lever is physically squeezed (`>= 10.0 mm`) for `>= 2.0 s` cumulative at `3.5–8.0 PSI` onto the exposed dandelion crown. Spray time only accrues while the nozzle is held `2.5-12.0 cm` above and aimed `<= 5.5 cm` from the crown, so the whole `>= 2.0 s` dose is delivered by the `12 deg` cone (`1.1-5.1 cm` footprint) onto the `9.0 cm` rosette, not a passing flick; on the real card this shows as a blue deposit over the crown card, with zero wetting of the hosta leaf.
- **1.00**: also at the end the shut-off valve is closed (`lever <= 2.0 mm`), the wand rests released in its twin U-saddle holster (`within 3.0 cm` and `<= 18 deg` of `(20.5, -31.0, 17.5)`), the hosta leaf has settled back (`<= 12 deg` of rest), and both grippers remain open (`>= 5.5 cm`), `>= 10.0 cm` clear of tank, tray, wand, holster, and planter, moving `<= 1.5 cm` over the final `1.0 s`.

## Simulation fidelity
Rigid and articulated PhysX at `240 Hz` (`20 Hz` control). The unclamped `1.16 kg` `Chapin 20000` tank carries a vertical `Z` prismatic plunger (`13.8 N` Viton cup-seal drag on intake upstroke — lifting the `11.4 N` tank if unbraced — plus live air-pressure compression force on downstroke; isothermal Boyle's law adds `+0.584 PSI` per `11.5 cm` stroke onto the live `Winters PFQ800` `0–15 PSI` dial needle). Inside the planter, the PE hosta shoot (`14 mm` petiole stem + `7.6 x 10.4 cm` `TeeJet`-clad blade, zero rear lever) rides an `X`-axis revolute crown hinge (`0.45 N·m/rad` return spring). On the `180 g` wand (resting passively by gravity on open twin U-saddles), `wand_lever` is a separate colliding rigid body on a `Y`-axis prismatic joint (`0..12.0 mm`, `350 N/m` return spring): a light `~25.6 mm` two-pad carry grip holds `spray_wand` with `lever <= 2 mm` (valve shut), while squeezing the parallel fingers closed physically drives `wand_lever` inward `>= 10 mm` to emit a `12 deg` raycast spray cone checked against the full `7.6 x 10.4 cm` hosta blade before crediting the dandelion target. Grasp model (sim stand-in for pad friction; no rigid weld): a 6-axis spring-damper joint between gripper and the tank lug, pump T-handle, hosta petiole or shut-off handle engages only when both finger pads touch it (PhysX contact on each pad; on the shut-off handle one pad bears on the handle body and the other on its trigger lever) and the jaws are commanded closed past their measured opening (on the spring-loaded shut-off handle, whose lever gives under the pads, commanded to within `0.5 mm` of it), with the pad midpoint on that handle region. It is anchored at the current relative pose, so nothing snaps; its springs are `20,000 N/m` linear and `1.5 N m/rad` rotational on the round T-bar, lug and petiole (`4.0 N m/rad` on the flat-faced shut-off handle), because the `2 mm` neoprene jaw sleeves let a held handle rock a few degrees in the pads. Each axis is force-limited to what two rubber pads can transmit: `50 N` pull (2 pads x mu `0.5` x `50 N` squeeze), `0.50 N m` twist about the pad normal, `1.50 N m` tipping (twist cap = pull cap x `10 mm` mean pad-patch radius; tipping cap = 2 x `50 N` x `15 mm` half pad length). When the load needed exceeds a cap, the part slips in the jaws (the anchor moves) and the slip is logged. It releases when the jaws are commanded open, or (except on the shut-off handle) when both-pad contact is lost for `5` control steps. Sources: YAM gripper effort limit `50 N` per finger (i2rt linear_4310 actuator); rubber pad friction mu `0.5`; pad patch ~`20 x 30 mm`.

## Fabrication of the two printed parts
- Hosta crown clevis (PLA, `24 x 20 x 18 mm`): a block screwed to the planter's catch deck with a `14.5 mm` blind socket that takes the frond's `14 mm` stem base (one drop of cyanoacrylate), a `4 mm` cross-hole on the `X` axis for the stainless hinge pin, and two printed stop faces at `0 deg` (leaf resting over the weed) and `82 deg`. The `McMaster-Carr 9271K33` torsion spring sits on the pin, one leg against the block and one taped to the stem, giving the `0.45 N·m/rad` return. Any artificial hosta frond with a `~14 mm` stem and a blade trimmed to `7.6 x 10.4 cm` fits; the blade lies `2.4 cm` above the dandelion crown at rest.
- Twin U-saddle holster (PETG): two posts `16 x 46 x 163 mm` at `x = 15.6` and `25.4 cm` on `y = -31.0`, each topped by two `4 x 26 mm` cheeks leaving a `38 mm` slot, plus a `6 mm` rear stop at `x = 14.3`; the wand drops in from above and rests by gravity.
